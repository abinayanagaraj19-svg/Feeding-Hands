# 🤝 Feeding Hands

### Connecting People with Food, Hope & Humanity

**Feeding Hands** is a responsive NGO website designed to showcase an organisation's mission, social impact, volunteering opportunities, donation initiatives, events, team members, testimonials, and contact information.

The project focuses on creating a clean, accessible, and engaging digital platform that helps an NGO connect with volunteers, donors, and people who want to contribute to social causes.

---

## 🌟 Features

* 🏠 **Responsive Landing Page** – Engaging homepage introducing the organisation and its mission.
* ❤️ **About & Mission** – Information about the NGO, its vision, and social initiatives.
* 🌍 **Social Impact** – Highlights the organisation's contribution to the community.
* 🙋 **Volunteer Opportunities** – Allows visitors to explore ways to get involved.
* 💰 **Donation Section** – Provides information about supporting the organisation financially.
* 📅 **Events** – Displays upcoming and past NGO events and activities.
* 👥 **Our Team** – Introduces the people working behind the organisation.
* 💬 **Testimonials** – Showcases experiences and feedback from volunteers and supporters.
* 📩 **Contact Section** – Provides ways for visitors to connect with the organisation.
* 📱 **Mobile-Friendly Design** – Optimised for desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
* ✨ **Animations & Interactions** – Smooth animations and interactive UI elements.
* 📝 **Interactive Forms** – User-friendly forms for communication and engagement.
* 🧭 **Responsive Navigation** – Navigation adapts to different screen sizes.
